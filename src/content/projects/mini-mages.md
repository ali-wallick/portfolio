The tournament runs across three mini-games, first to five points:

- **Summon:** Rock-paper-scissors against an evil wizard in the center.
- **Potions:** Simon, played by pouring ingredients into a cauldron in the right order.
- **Dragon Battle:** The iPhones become tilt controllers for dragons that shoot fireballs at each
  other.

The client, a local iPhone app startup, wanted the game as a demo of iPad-and-iPhone connectivity to
show its own customers. I built most of the non-game scenes: the connection flow, overworld, and win
screen. I also added the scoring, the round-robin game picker, and the UI tying it together. On the
design side, I made the final calls on the game’s concept and each mini-game, working with the rest
of the team.
