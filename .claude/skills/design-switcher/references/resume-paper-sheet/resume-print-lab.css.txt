/*
 * SCAFFOLDING — not shipped. Deleted before this branch merges.
 *
 * The résumé's printed page, reproduced on screen so its treatments can be
 * compared live (#235). Two halves:
 *
 *   1. `.lab-sheet` — a transcription of `src/styles/resume.css`'s
 *      `@media print` block: the same token pins, on the sheet instead of
 *      `:root`, and the same rules, scoped under `.lab-sheet`. What base.css
 *      contributes on paper it contributes here too, so the cascade is the
 *      print cascade. (The one thing print sets on `body` — face, size,
 *      leading, colour, `font-variant-numeric` — is set on the sheet
 *      directly, since a variable re-pinned below `body` cannot reach a
 *      property `body` already resolved.) The instrument's honesty is
 *      measured, not assumed: the sheet's `.resume` height is checked against
 *      `/resume` under real print emulation before anything is judged on it.
 *
 *   2. Candidate overrides keyed on `data-*` written to `.lab-sheet` by
 *      `src/scripts/resume-print-lab.ts`. A settled axis is deleted by
 *      removing its attribute selectors.
 *
 * Nothing here is a `byteHashedFiles` input, and `resume.css` is not
 * imported by the route. Raw hex is unavoidable in the sheet half because
 * paper's palette IS raw hex in the print block; the candidate inks are the
 * thing under comparison and carry their contrast in the panel's labels.
 */
